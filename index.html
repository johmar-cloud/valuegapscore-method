How the value gap score works

The score compares a stock's multiples with its peer group, then adjusts for operating trend and price momentum. Every number below can be moved. Worked examples come from the 12 October 2026 weekly scan.

1 The value gap

How far a stock's multiples sit from its peers'.

VGap = 100 × ln( peer median multiple ÷ own multiple )

The gap is worked out for three multiples: P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF. The median of the three is the VGap. EV/EBIT is shown for reference only. A positive gap means the stock is cheaper than its peers. A negative gap means it is dearer.

The gate

±22.3 is a 20% discount or premium, since 100 × ln(1.25) = 22.3. Inside the gate there is no base signal. Reading the scale: +30 means the peer median is about 35% above own. −30 means it is about 26% below. +69.3 means the stock trades at half the peer median.

Integrity filters

Spread is the largest leg gap minus the smallest. Above 80 the multiples disagree about the company. Peer dispersion above 0.6 means the peer group is incoherent, so a discount to it is weak evidence. With only two usable legs, the median is their average and one broken leg poisons it.

2 The VG_Score builder

Base signal plus an operating modifier plus a momentum modifier. Range −2 to +2 in half steps.

Blended means 60% last twelve months and 40% latest quarter.

−2 short candidate0+2 long candidate

Base

+1 when VGap is above +22.3, −1 when below −22.3, otherwise 0.

Operating modifier

Margin band: +1 above +1pp, −1 below −1pp, 0 between. Revenue band: +1 above 10% growth, 0 from 0% to 10%, −1 below 0%. The sign of the mean of the two bands, times 0.5.

Momentum modifier

0 when the 1-month return is within ±3%. Otherwise ±0.5 with the sign of the move.

Modifiers carry the sign of the trend, not of the trade. For a short, a positive operating score offsets the negative base. Scale reading: ±2 ready, ±1.5 and ±1 mixed signals, ±0.5 not there yet, 0 no gap.

4 Z-score

How unusual a stock's move is against its group.

Z = ( stock 3M return − group typical 3M return ) ÷ group standard deviation

Z

0 means in line with the group. Beyond ±1 is unusual. Measured against the sector, Z below −1 raises the Orphan flag and above +1 the Leader flag. In the 12 October scan, NXP printed −1.22 and Airbnb +1.45.
